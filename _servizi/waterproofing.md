A leak is not fixed by repainting: it has to be traced and stopped at the source. Edil Cramor waterproofs terraces, balconies, flat roofs and exposed surfaces, choosing the solution that suits the substrate and its use: membrane, liquid waterproofing or a localised repair.

## When you need it
- Damp stains on the ceiling below terraces or balconies
- Plaster that swells or crumbles
- Old, cracked or lifted membranes
- Standing water caused by poor slopes
- Lifting tiles and open joints

## Works included
- Waterproofing of terraces and balconies
- Waterproofing of flat roofs
- Leak detection and repair
- Replacement of waterproofing membranes
- Liquid waterproofing
- Sealing and localised repairs

## Our approach
Before choosing the remedy we check where the water gets in and the condition of the screed, slopes, upstands and drains. If the membrane is still sound a localised repair is often enough; when it is spent, redoing the waterproofing over the whole surface avoids coming back to the same problem. After laying it we restore flooring, joints and sealants.
