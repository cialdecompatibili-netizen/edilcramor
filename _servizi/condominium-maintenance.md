Property managers need a reliable contractor who follows the works from assessment to completion. Edil Cramor handles routine and extraordinary maintenance of common areas with a single point of contact, clear quotes and documentation of the works.

## When you need it
- Reports from residents about leaks, detachments or settlement
- Works approved at the owners' meeting
- Scheduled maintenance of facades, roofs and stairwells
- A technical report and photos needed for the minutes
- Urgent works on common areas

## Works included
- Routine and extraordinary maintenance
- Restoration of stairs, landings and entrance halls
- Garage and car park maintenance
- Restoration of technical rooms
- Masonry and flooring repairs
- Maintenance of courtyards and common areas
- Restoration works on common parts

## Our approach
After the survey we provide a report with photographic documentation and a detailed quote, ready to present at the owners' meeting. During the works we keep the manager updated on progress and, at the end, we leave documentation of the works carried out.

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
