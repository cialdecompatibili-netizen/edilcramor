Before spending money you need to know what has to be done. An Edil Cramor survey produces a clear report and a photographic record of the site, including drone surveys, useful for making decisions, for the quote and for communication at owners' meetings.

## When you need it
- Before a major quote
- To check the condition of facades and roofing
- To document damage
- To prepare a condominium resolution
- To compare quotes on a clear basis

## Works included
- Technical surveys
- Inspections and visual checks of properties
- Descriptive technical reports
- Photographic reports
- Documentation of the state of the site
- Surveys and inspections by drone
- Photographic documentation before, during and after works

## Our approach
We collect photos of the critical points, describe the problem and list the recommended works in order of priority. Technical activities that require a licensed professional, such as expert appraisals and designs, are carried out through qualified professionals.

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
