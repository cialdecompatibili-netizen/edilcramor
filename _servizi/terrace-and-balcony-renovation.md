Terraces and balconies are among the most exposed parts of a building: water, frost and sun wear them down. When the flooring, screed and waterproofing have deteriorated, a full renovation prevents leaks and restores safety and appearance.

## When you need it
- Lifting or broken tiles
- Leaks into the flat below
- Wrong slopes causing standing water
- Exposed reinforcement in balcony edges
- Parapets to repair or replace

## Works included
- Complete renovation of terraces and balconies
- Demolition and rebuilding of screeds
- New flooring
- Waterproofing
- Restoration of balcony edges and soffits
- Restoration of parapets and railings
- Replacement of thresholds and drip edges

## Our approach
The work follows a precise sequence: removing damaged parts, restoring the screed with the right slopes, waterproofing, new flooring and sealing. Each stage is checked, because it is the correct order of the layers that makes a terrace last.

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
