Falling plaster, crumbling cornices and damaged balconies are a risk for passers-by and residents. Making the area safe removes the danger at once; the repair then brings the damaged part back to proper condition.

## When you need it
- Detaching plaster or concrete
- Exposed, rusting reinforcement bars
- Cracked cornices and balcony edges
- Unstable parapets or balconies
- Reports of danger to passers-by

## Works included
- Securing of facades and balconies
- Removal of loose plaster and hazardous elements
- Restoration of cornices and balcony edges
- Protection and safety measures
- Repair of damaged parts
- Urgent works in hazardous situations

## Our approach
We first deal with the unstable parts, removing loose material, and document the condition found with photos. Then comes the repair: treating the reinforcement, rebuilding the concrete cover, plaster and finishes. This way the job is a real fix, not just a temporary one.

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
