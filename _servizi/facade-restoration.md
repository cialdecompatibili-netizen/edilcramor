The facade protects the building and defines its image. When plaster and cladding deteriorate, we step in to stop the decay, remove the damaged parts and bring the surface back to a safe, tidy condition.

## When you need it
- Plaster that cracks, crumbles or comes away
- Damaged balcony edges and cornices
- Damp stains and exterior mould
- Faded paintwork
- Balconies with exposed reinforcement

## Works included
- Facade renewal and upgrading
- Restoration of balcony edges and cornices
- Restoration of external plaster
- External painting
- Restoration of balconies and parapets
- Removal and repair of deteriorated elements

## Our approach
We start with a survey, report and photos, including drone images for high areas. We then choose repair cycles suited to the substrate: repairs, plastering, protective coatings and painting. The work can be done with scaffolding or on ropes, depending on height and surface.

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
