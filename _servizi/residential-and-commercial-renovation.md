Whether it is a flat, common areas or commercial premises, a successful renovation depends on planning and control. Edil Cramor follows the building works from survey to handover, with qualified craftsmen and a single point of contact.

## When you need it
- Renovating a home or a shop
- Repairs after damage
- Rebuilding masonry, plaster and finishes
- Upgrading and adapting spaces
- Extraordinary maintenance

## Works included
- Full and partial renovations
- Residential and commercial renovations
- Demolition and reconstruction
- Masonry and partition walls
- Plastering, skim coats and painting
- New floors and wall coverings
- Restoration and damp-proofing of masonry

## Our approach
We agree the works, schedule and quote with you before starting. During the job we coordinate the phases to reduce disruption and surprises. Activities reserved to licensed professionals, such as designs and permits, are carried out through specialised professionals.

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
