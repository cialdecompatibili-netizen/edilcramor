To truly judge the condition of a facade or roof you have to look at it up close. A technical rope-access survey lets us inspect every point at height, even hard-to-reach ones, without erecting scaffolding.

## When you need it
- Cracks or detachments visible from below
- Checking high cornices, balcony edges and balconies
- Checks after bad weather
- Before quoting works at height
- Scheduled periodic maintenance

## Works included
- Technical surveys at height with rope access
- Inspection of facades, balconies and cornices
- Visual check of the condition of building elements
- Detection of detachments, cracks and critical points
- Inspection of roofs, gutters and downpipes
- Checks of hard-to-reach points
- Photographic documentation of the problems found
- Descriptive technical reports on the state of the site

## Our approach
A specialised operator inspects the surfaces up close and documents the critical points with photos and video. A report follows with the priorities for action, so decisions rest on concrete data. If needed we can deal at once with any unstable parts.

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
