Rope-access building works let us work on facades, cornices, balconies and roofs without erecting scaffolding. They suit targeted jobs or hard-to-reach areas, where scaffolding and platforms would cost too much or take too long.

## When you need it
- Works limited to part of a facade
- Checks and repairs on high or hard-to-reach areas
- Maintenance of cornices, balcony edges and gutters
- Narrow spaces or public ground that must stay clear
- Urgent safety works

## Works included
- Facade restoration with rope access
- Restoration of balcony edges and cornices
- Gutter and downpipe maintenance
- Sealing and waterproofing
- Plaster restoration
- Localised works at height
- Securing of loose or hazardous elements

## Our approach
We work with trained operators, dedicated equipment and safety procedures specific to work at height. Rope access is not always the right choice: for large surfaces or long jobs scaffolding may be better. During the survey we assess both options and recommend the best one for safety, time and cost.

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
