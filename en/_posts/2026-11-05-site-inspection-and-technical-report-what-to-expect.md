Before talking about costs you need to understand the real state of the property. That is why every job starts with a site inspection.

## How it works
A technician checks the areas concerned by eye: facades, balconies, cornices, roofs and common areas. Where needed, the check is made at height on ropes or by drone, to see hard-to-reach points too.

## What the report contains
- A description of the state of the site
- Critical points found, such as detachments, cracks and leaks
- A photographic report of the problems
- Proposed works

## What it is for
The report is a clear document to share with the manager and present at the meeting. It helps decide with concrete data and compare proposals.

## The next step
Based on the report we prepare a clear quote before starting any work.

## How the inspection works
The reported points and at-risk areas are inspected, with photos, measurements and, if needed, drone or rope-access surveys. The inspection is not binding: it serves to understand the real condition of the building.

## What the report contains
The report describes the problems found, shows captioned photos and lists the recommended works in order of priority. It is the basis for an itemised, comparable and clear quote, and for communication at owners' meetings.

## Frequently asked questions
### How long until I receive the report?
It depends on the size of the building and the surveys needed; we tell you at the inspection.

### Does the report replace an expert appraisal?
No: appraisals and designs are for licensed professionals, with whom we can work.
